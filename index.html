01 — The villa

Kvarner tradition, quietly modern.

Villa Nika is a five-star holiday home in Kras, the highest village on the island of Krk. Built in a blend of contemporary and rustic style, it gives you privacy, luxury and calm — while the island’s beaches, towns and sights are only 10–15 minutes away by car.

The interior follows the tradition of the Kvarner coast: wooden beams on the walls, wooden furniture in the living room and parquet floors in the bedrooms give every room warmth. Yet most of the day is spent outside — in the large garden and on the sunny pool terrace.

Woods, meadows and silence all around — the sea seven kilometres away.

02 — Outdoors

Days here are spent outside.

The heart of the garden is the pool, framed by sunbeds and parasols. At the far end stands a stone outdoor grill and, a little further on, a covered terrace with a shaded dining table. All of it is hidden by greenery, far from the noise.

03 — The konoba

The old cellar, reborn.

Guests’ favourite room has a story. In island houses, the konoba was the cool room where food and drinks were traditionally kept. At Villa Nika it has become a private retreat — with a sauna and a mini gym.

08 — Location

The highest village on Krk.

Kras lies in the green interior of the island, in the municipality of Dobrinj — woods and meadows made for cycling and walks. Some of the most beautiful bays of the northern Adriatic are about ten minutes away by car, and wine lovers shouldn’t miss Vrbnik, home of the local white wine Žlahtina.
